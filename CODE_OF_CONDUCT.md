# Code of conduct

We want OpenBindings to be an enjoyable, professional place to collaborate.

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Disagree on ideas without attacking people.
- Do not harass, threaten, or disclose private information.

Report unacceptable behavior to `hello@openbindings.com` or Matthew Clevenger
[@clevengermatt](https://github.com/clevengermatt). Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled as privately as practical.

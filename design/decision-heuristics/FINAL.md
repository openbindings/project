## What changed

The procedure now distinguishes applying authority, choosing a local design,
proposing shared doctrine, and authorizing an action. Inferred choices can
remain in a working draft while review is pending. Confidence follows the
actual premises; it cannot be raised by quoting a principle heading or an
earlier loop's own text. Options are compared through explicit stages, with
a defined fallback when the evidence does not distinguish them.

Consumer evidence admits essential first-use work and demonstrated
conveniences without allowing loop-generated examples to manufacture demand.
Reopening corrects reasoning mistakes and new evidence, resets materially
affected acceptance, and rechecks dependents. Partial acceptance must leave
a coherent landing artifact. Grades remain attached to the exact draft seen
by reviewers.

The examples separate complete decision demonstrations from unresolved
evaluator packages. The baseline audit corrected inherited claims about
unbounded conversion, Close having no observable job, numeric-kind rules,
and a universal JSON-decoder representation. Recommendations were not forced
to retain their old verdicts after their supporting claims failed.

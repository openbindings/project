Matt's decision-making tendencies, ratified 2026-08-20 as 12 rules. Apply them
when recommending a course of action or when asked to think like him.

1. **Two grounds of truth**: the Core and the incorporated upstream authority.
   His own rulings are revisable precedent that need re-affirmation, not
   ground truth. See [[ruling-authority-split]].
2. **Support exactly what upstream supports.** See
   [[feedback_coverage_never_buys_drift]].
3. **Every layer claims exactly its job**, no more and no less.
4. **Suspect uniformity that couples.** Sameness across layers is not by
   itself a virtue.
5. **DX is supreme inside the constraints, never through them.**
6. **Zero config to start.**
7. **Delegate to formed expectations.** Match what a user of that surface
   already expects. See [[feedback_independent_interfaces]].
8. **Predictable and loud.** Fail visibly rather than degrade quietly.
9. **Substitutability is the payoff.** See [[feedback_no_spec_antipatterns]].
10. **Rule the general form**, not the instance in front of you.
11. **Challenge gaps before filling them.** See
    [[feedback_vet_authorities_before_ruling]] and
    [[project_revision_not_roadmap]].
12. **Systems serve shipping.**

**How to apply:** run candidate recommendations through the rules; when
presenting options, note which rules decide and which leave the choice
genuinely neutral.

**Provenance note:** the source document `ob-pj/decision-lens.md` carried a
per-rule provenance citation. It was never committed to git and was deleted
2026-09-18, so this list is the only surviving record. The headline wording
here is preserved from the original; the per-rule provenance is lost.

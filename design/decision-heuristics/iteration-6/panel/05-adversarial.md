The procedure substantially resists authority laundering, confidence inflation, stale acceptance, and reopening by repetition. I found one narrow provenance ambiguity that can admit manufactured demand. It does not defeat the separate acceptance and landing gates.

## Demonstrated ambiguity: committed synthetic callers can manufacture demand

**Input.** An authorized loop is considering an optional public recovery helper H. Existing operations already perform the supported task. No independent caller has requested H, and the brief does not name a new integration requiring it. The loop writes and commits an executable demonstration application that repeats a six-step recovery sequence in eight places, then cites that commit as H's consumer. The repetition and the shorter H calling path are real, but the application exists solely to demonstrate the proposal.

**Conflicting permitted readings.** The Consumer definition explicitly accepts “calling code at a commit”; its explicit independence qualifier attaches to an issue. It excludes “Reviewer sketches and issues filed by the loop,” without defining whether a complete, runnable, committed demonstration remains a sketch.

* Read the application as calling code at a commit: the helper has a consumer plus demonstrated repetition costs. Assuming the other stages are neutral, select and provisionally apply H, inferred/pending, just as the demanded-convenience fixture selects its helper.
* Read the same application as a reviewer sketch despite its executable, committed form: it supplies an argument but no independent demand. Defer H under rule 11.

The disagreement is about the provenance gate, before design preferences are compared. Making a sketch executable should not silently change whether it establishes demand. The examples correctly require an independently written integration issue, but do not settle the equivalent code case.

**Minimal fix.** Extend the origin exclusion to every evidence form: “Calling code, demonstrations, fixtures, tests, or issues created by the loop solely to support its proposal do not establish independent demand. Calling code implementing an independently authorized caller requirement may count; record that requirement and the code's provenance.” This preserves new integrations and essential first-use work while closing the format conversion.

## Attacks that the current text blocks

**Authority and scope laundering.** Reclassifying a forbidden class behavior as a member surface fails step 0's explicit governing-contract check. Informative schemas and reference implementations cannot overturn normative text. Missing authority or an inaccessible relevant reader holds the affected edit. A loop-authored responsibility cannot become an established constraint simply by surviving an iteration.

**Inflated confidence.** A ratified principle heading is insufficient for high confidence. The operative test needs recorded acceptance, and every material deciding or neutralizing premise is classified. An accepted local precedent remains a precedent unless the acceptance expressly adopts a general test. These provisions defeat the obvious “the previous iteration now proves it” maneuver.

**Stale acceptance.** The dependency closure before landing and explicit reset of materially affected acceptance prevent an accepted example from carrying a pending return-type change. The partial-batch example demonstrates both the prohibited landing and a valid rebase onto the retained baseline.

**Gratuitous reopening.** A changed sample key is explicitly a repeat. Reopening must challenge a recorded premise, expose a mistake, change a dependency, or follow maintainer direction. Demonstrated procedural mistakes can reopen unchanged evidence; the stability rule preserves only a still-valid answer. I do not treat it as permission to preserve a demonstrated misapplication.

## Preferences and possible extensions, not demonstrated defects

The final Unicode fallback is wording-sensitive: two otherwise tied designs can switch order if their descriptions begin “Choose…” versus “Adopt…”. Freezing prose ensures reproducibility for that frozen record, not resistance to an author who knows the comparator. However, the procedure expressly calls this arbitrary, claims no design merit, and still requires acceptance before landing. A canonical serialization or externally fixed identifiers would be an optional stronger neutrality guarantee, not a correctness repair under the stated guarantee.

The evidence ledger is demanding, but its detail supports the adversarial protections. A compact reusable record template could improve adoption without changing the decision rules. The unresolved evaluator cases appropriately advertise their missing comparisons rather than presenting incumbent retention as completed selection.
